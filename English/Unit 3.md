
## Talking About the Present

Some words describe actions happening **right now**:

- **working**
- **eating**
- **watching**
- **playing**
- **talking**

### Examples:
- I **am working**. (I am working now.)
- She **is playing** basketball. (She is playing now.)
- You **are watching** a party. (You are watching now.)
- He **is eating** a sandwich. (He is eating now.)
- They **are taking** the bus. (They are taking it now.)

---

## Talking About the Future

We use the present continuous and "going to" to talk about things that will happen **later**.

### Examples:
- They **are playing** basketball tomorrow.
- She **is watching** a movie later.
- He **is arriving** in December.

---

## Asking Questions About the Future

To ask about the future, use **"What is / are ... doing?"**

### Examples:
- **You ask:** What **is** she **doing** later?  
  **I answer:** She **is playing** a game later.
- **You ask:** What **are** you **doing** tomorrow?  
  **I answer:** I **am going to** the movies tomorrow.
- **You ask:** What **are** we **doing** in May?  
  **I answer:** We **are going to** a party in May.

---

## Talking About Planned Actions

When we have planned actions, we often use **"going to"**.

### Examples:
- I **am going to play** basketball.
- He **is going to catch** the ball.
- She **is going to throw** the ball.
- They **are going to begin** the game.
- We **are going to finish** the game.

---

## Using Time Expressions

We use specific time expressions to talk about the past, present, and future.

### Talking About the Present:
- **now**
- **at the moment**
- **right now**

### Talking About the Future:
- **tomorrow**
- **later**
- **next week / next month / next year**
- **in December / in May / in 2025**
- **at 7:30 a.m. / at night**
- **on Saturday / on Friday, May 17th**

### Talking About the Past:
- **yesterday**
- **last week / last month / last year**
- **at 6 p.m. last night**
- **on Thursday / on February 14th**
- **in the morning / in the afternoon**

---

## Talking About the Past

Some words change when talking about past events:

### Examples:
- **I walk** on the street. → **I walked** on the street.
- **She goes** to the store. → **She went** to the store.
- **They take** the bus. → **They took** the bus.

---

## More Examples with Time Expressions

### Talking About the Past:
- He **ate** dinner at 6 p.m. last night.
- I **bought** a ticket on Thursday.
- They **arrived** in the morning.
- She **went** to the restaurant on Saturday, February 14th.
- Matt **played** basketball at 4:15 in the afternoon.

### Talking About the Future:
- We **are watching** a movie at 2:30 p.m. on Friday.
- He **is going to play** basketball at night on July 19th.
- I **am going to go** to the restaurant on Friday, May 17th.
- Lisa **is playing** basketball at 11 o’clock in the morning.

---

### Summary
- Use **present continuous** for things happening **now**.
- Use **present continuous or "going to"** for **future** plans.
- Use **past tense** for things that already happened.
- Use **time expressions** to specify when something happens.
