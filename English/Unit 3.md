## Lesson 2

### Remember: 
**working, eating, watching, playing, talking**  

You use these words to talk about things you are doing now.  

You say:  
- I am working. I am working now.  
- She is playing basketball. She is playing now.  
- You are watching a party. You are watching now.  
- He is eating a sandwich. He is eating now.  
- They are taking the bus. They are taking it now.  

### Talking about the future  
You talk about things happening later. They are not happening now.  

You say:  
- They are playing basketball tomorrow.  
- She is watching a movie later.  
- He is arriving in December.  

### Asking questions about the future  
You ask questions about the future. I answer questions about the future.  

- **You ask:** What is she doing later?  
  **I answer:** She is playing a game later.  
- **You ask:** What are you doing tomorrow?  
  **I answer:** I am going to the movies tomorrow.  
- **You ask:** What are we doing in May?  
  **I answer:** We're going to a party in May.  

### Talking about the future  
You talk about things you are doing in the future.  

You say:  
- We are seeing a movie tomorrow.  
- I am eating at a restaurant tomorrow.  

You talk about what you are going to do in the future.  

You say:  
- She is going to sing. She is not singing now.  
- We are going to play a game. We are not playing now.  

### Talking about planned actions  
You know many new words. You can talk about what people are going to do.  

You say:  
- I am going to play basketball.  
- He is going to catch the ball.  
- She is going to throw the ball.  
- They are going to begin the game.  
- We are going to finish the game.  

### Using future time expressions  
You talk about what people are going to do with future time expressions.  

You say:  
- I am going to see the movie tomorrow.  
- We are going to watch the basketball game later.  
- She is going to arrive on Friday.
