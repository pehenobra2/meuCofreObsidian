## 1. O que são e para que servem

### 1.1 Past simple

- Uso principal:
	- Ações concluídas em um momento específico do passado
		- `I visited Paris last year.`
	- Sequência de eventos no passado
		- `She finished work, went home, and cooked dinner.`
	- Hábitos no passado (agora já não ocorrem)
		- `When I was a child, I played football every weekend.`
- Formação:
	- Verbos regulares: verbo + -ed
		- Work -> worked
		- Play -> played
	- Verbos irregulares: 
		- Forma própria:
			- go -> went
			- have -> had
	- Negativa:
		- did + not + verbo(base)
			- I did not see that movie
	- Interrogativa: did + sujeito + verbo ?
		- Did you watch the game?
- Palavras-chave:
	- yesterday
	- last week/month/year
	- ago
	- in 2010
	- when
	- then

### 1.2 Past continuous

- Uso principal:
	- Ação em progresso em um momento específico do passado
		- At 8 p.m. last night, I was studying.
	- Ação interrompida por outra ação (Past Simple)
		- I was watching TV when the phone rang.
	- Duas ações paralelas no passado
		- She was cooking while he was cleaning.
	- Descrição de cenário ou fundo em histórias
		- It was raining and people were running for cover
- Formação:
	- was/were + verbo+ing
		- I/he/she/it was working
		- You/We/they were working
	- Negativa: was/were + not + verbo+ing
		- They weren't listening
	- Interrogativa: was/were + sujeito + verbo+ing?
		- Were you sleeping?
- Palavras-chave:
	- While
	- When
	- at (hour)
	- all day
	- the whole time
	- as

## 2. Contexto em português

- **Past Simple** : Indica ações completas ou sequências no passado, normalmente associadas a datas, períodos ou marcadores (yesterday, two days ago).
- **Past Continuous**: Enfatiza que algo estava em andamento naquele instante ou servia de cenário para outra ação.

**Comparação**:
- I watched a film at 9 p.m. -> Past Simple (ação pontual)
- I was watching a film at 9 p.m. -> Past Continuous (ação em progresso naquele momento)

## 4. Exercícios

### Exercício 1: Preenchimento de lacunas  
Preencha as lacunas com a forma correta do verbo em **past simple** ou **past continuous**.

1. She ____ (visit) her grandparents last weekend.  
2. While I ____ (walk) to work, it ____ (start) to rain.  
3. They ____ (not / watch) the news when I called.  
4. At 9 p.m. yesterday, I ____ (have) dinner.  
5. We ____ (study) English when the power ____ (go) out.  

Respostas:

1. She visited her grandparents last weekend.
2. While I was walking to work it was started to rain.
3. They didn't watch the news, when I called.
4. At 9 p.m. yesterday, I had dinner.
5. We were studying English when the power went out.

### Exercício 2: Formação de frases  
Monte frases completas a partir das palavras dadas. Use **past simple** ou **past continuous** conforme indicado.

1. (He / finish / the report / before / the meeting) [sequência de ações]  
2. (I / not / understand / why / she / leave / in a hurry) [interrupção]  
3. (They / play / football / all afternoon) [ação em progresso]  
4. (We / arrive / at the airport / at 6 a.m.) [ação pontual]  
5. (She / write / emails / when / the phone / ring) [ação interrompida]  

Respostas:

1. He finished the report before the meeting.
2. I didn't understand why she left in a hurry.
3. They were playing football all afternoon.
4. We arrived at the airport at 6 a.m.
5. She was writing emails when the phone rang.

### Exercício 3: Misturando com Present Perfect  
Preencha as lacunas com **past simple**, **past continuous** ou **present perfect**, conforme apropriado:

1. I ____ (live) here since 2010.  
2. When I ____ (arrive), they ____ (already / leave).  
3. She ____ (not / finish) her homework last night.  
4. They ____ (travel) around Europe when they ____ (meet) him.  
5. We ____ (never / see) such a beautiful sunset before. 

Respostas:

1. I have lived here since 2010-> Present Perfect
	- Why: "Since 2010" indicates a situation continuing  to the present, so use present perfect
2. When I arrived, they had already left.
	- Past perfect ("had left") shows the eralier past action
3. She didn't finish her home work last night.
4. They were traveling around Europe when they were met him.
5. We had never seen such a beautiful sunset before.
	- "before" indicates the experience up to that past moment - use Past perfect
